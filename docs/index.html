About

Systems That Hold Real Money.

I own systems end to end: the API, the web, the app, the money and the release. On Mybizna that means one deployment serving every seller, with tenancy enforced by the runtime rather than remembered by the author, ledgers that are append-only and reconciled against the processor, and eleven architecture checks in CI whose counts can only fall.

When a model touches a business, it goes through typed tools gated by risk: every number it reports is a tool result, and anything that matters becomes a proposal a person approves. Every figure on this site was measured from the repositories in November 2026.
